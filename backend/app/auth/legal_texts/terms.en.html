LaMP terms of use

Version 2026-11-28. A draft for testing: a lawyer checks it before any real shop's data is used (build plan R12).

1. What LaMP is

LaMP helps pharmacy owners keep track of supplier bills, stock, cash and what customers owe. Families who agree get payment reminders by SMS. LaMP is built by team IUT_GrandHackAuto for AppQuest 2.0, and its SMS, sign-in codes and charges run on Banglalink's Applink platform.

2. Your account

  • You sign in with a Banglalink number and a code Banglalink sends to it. Keep your phone and the code to yourself.
  • You can see every phone and browser signed in to your shop, and sign any of them out, in Settings.
  • LaMP is for pharmacy owners and managers aged 18 or over, in Bangladesh.

3. Your data is yours

You can download everything LaMP holds about your shop, or delete your account, at any time in Settings. How we handle data is in the privacy policy.

4. Your customers

  • A family joins SMS reminders only by texting LAMP and their card code to 21213 from their own phone, or, if that route is on, by agreeing at your counter. Never add anyone who hasn't agreed.
  • LaMP keeps no customer names and no medicines. A card shows amounts only.
  • Any family can stop at any time by texting LAMP STOP to 21213.

5. Figures, photos and AI

  • LaMP's figures come from what you enter and scan. Reading a bill photo can go wrong, so you check and confirm every bill before it is saved.
  • LaMP's answers and suggestions help you decide; they are not financial, legal or medical advice. Your decisions are yours.

6. Paid features

  • LaMP Plus costs BDT 50 a month plus VAT, taken from your Banglalink balance by Banglalink after you confirm with a code. It continues until you cancel in the app.
  • A credit record costs BDT 10 each time, confirmed the same way.
  • Nothing is charged without your confirmation code. Charges and refunds follow Banglalink's and Applink's rules.

7. Fair use

Don't use LaMP to break the law, to message people who haven't agreed, or to get into another shop's data. We may suspend an account that does.

8. Availability

LaMP is in testing. We work to keep it running and your data safe, but we can't promise it will never stop or never make a mistake. To the extent the law allows, we aren't liable for losses caused by an outage or an error.

9. Changes

When these terms change, the app tells you and asks you to accept the new version before you go on.

10. Help

Use Help, then "Report a problem", in the app. We reply within 24 hours. These terms follow the laws of Bangladesh.
