LaMP privacy policy

Version 2026-11-28. A draft for testing: a lawyer checks it before any real shop's data is used (build plan R12).

1. What we collect

From pharmacy owners:

  • your phone number, to sign you in (kept encrypted, and as a keyed hash for finding your account);
  • your shop's details and records: suppliers, bills, stock, cash, sales, and what each customer card owes;
  • the bill photos you scan;
  • the phones and browsers you sign in with (name, system and app version);
  • counts of which screens are used, without your shop, number or content.

About families: only a card code and what it owes. When a family texts 21213, Banglalink gives us an address for their phone (usually a masked id, not the number), and we keep it with the card code, the SMS status and delivery reports. We never keep a customer's name or medicines. If the family agrees at the counter instead, their number is kept encrypted and linked only to the card code.

Payments: we see whether a charge succeeded and its amount. We never see wallet or card details.

2. Why

  • to run LaMP for your shop, and keep each shop's data apart;
  • to send the SMS you or a family asked for, and the sign-in and payment codes Banglalink sends;
  • to charge for LaMP Plus and credit records, only after your confirmation code;
  • to keep LaMP secure (limits on tries, signed-in devices);
  • to improve LaMP from usage counts that identify no one.

3. Who else handles it

  • Banglalink, through Applink: SMS, sign-in codes, subscriptions and charges.
  • OpenAI: reads bill photos and answers your questions. We send them with OpenAI's setting not to store them.
  • Render: hosts our server and database in Singapore.
  • Sentry: error reports, sent without personal data.
  • A lender, only if you share your credit record with one, through a link you control and can revoke.

We don't sell data, and the LaMP app shows no ads.

4. Where and how it is kept safe

Our server and database are in Singapore, outside Bangladesh. Data is encrypted on the way, phone numbers are encrypted at rest, each shop is kept apart from every other, and only the LaMP team can reach the systems.

5. How long

  • your shop's data: while your account exists;
  • after you delete your account: erased after a 7-day undo window, and gone from backups within 30 days;
  • server logs: 30 days; the log of Applink calls: 90 days.

6. Your choices and rights

  • See and download all your data (Settings, then Export), correct it, or delete your account (Settings, then Delete).
  • Cancel LaMP Plus at any time. A family can stop SMS at any time with LAMP STOP to 21213.
  • Ask us anything about your data through Help, then "Report a problem". We reply within 24 hours.

We follow Bangladesh's personal data protection law: data is used only with consent, for the purposes above, and you can see, correct and delete it.

7. Changes

When this policy changes, the app tells you and asks you to accept the new version.
